world.txt: the map behind the time zone picker.

Land outlines come from Natural Earth's ne_110m_land and the time zone regions
from ne_10m_time_zones (one region per UTC offset), simplified by tools/mapgen.c.

Natural Earth is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Fetched from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (geojson/).
